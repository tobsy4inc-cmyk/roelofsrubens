How to commission a bespoke decoration

Brief

Share your idea with us - whether it’s a sketch, photo, reference, or simple outline. We can adapt existing designs or create something entirely bespoke for your venue.

Design

We’ll turn your brief into initial design proposals for you to review. From there, we refine the concept together until it feels just right.

Make

Once your design is approved, we move into production. Bespoke decorations are handcrafted to order, with timing shaped around the scale and details of your project.

Deliver

Your finished decoration is created exclusively for your venue or shop. It’s a unique piece designed to reflect your space and delight your visitors.

Looking for something bespoke?
