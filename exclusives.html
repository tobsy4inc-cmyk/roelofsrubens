How to commission an exclusive decoration

Brief

Everything starts with an idea, sketch, design brief or picture. We can add features to existing designs or develop a fully bespoke piece from scratch.

Design

We require at least two weeks from briefing to the first design proposals. You’ll receive a no-obligation design proposal with one or two evaluation sessions.

Make

For the first order we require a minimum of 100 decorations per design. Production lead times range from four to ten weeks depending on the order.

Deliver

Each exclusive design is made exclusively for the commissioning stockist. We won’t sell these products to a third party — they’re yours alone.

Looking for something bespoke?

We also create bespoke ceramic decorations for shops, venues, museums, cathedrals and other special places. If you have a commission in mind, we would love to hear from you.
