Bath Abbey

A handmade ceramic hanging decoration capturing the beauty of Bath Abbey, one of the finest examples of Perpendicular Gothic architecture in the West Country. Each piece is individually shaped, painted and glazed in our Berkshire studio, with fine brushwork detailing the Abbey’s famous fan-vaulted windows and ornate west front. Finished in traditional Delft blue with hand-threaded ceramic beads and a ribbon for hanging.
